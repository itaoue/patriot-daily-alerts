Most Advent calendars are gone the moment they are opened. The chocolate is eaten before breakfast; the little plastic toys are in the bin by New Year. This one works the other way round. Every evening in December you open one door, tip out a small bag of bricks, and build that day's ornament together: a snowman, a reindeer, a gingerbread house, a cardinal. Each one has a built-in hook, so it goes straight onto the tree.
